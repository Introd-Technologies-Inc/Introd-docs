## Controls at a glance

| Goal | Current control | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stop Google or Microsoft sync | Disconnect the provider in [Integrations](https://app.getintrod.ai/settings/integrations) | Future sync stops; imported Introd records remain |
| Stop the extension syncing LinkedIn | **Enable extension** off, or **Revoke sync permission**, on the extension Options page | No new sync runs; data already in Introd remains |
| Clear one Chrome installation | **Clear local data** on the extension Options page | The extension's local data is cleared; Introd's data remains |
| Close your account | **Request verified closure** in [Settings](https://app.getintrod.ai/settings) | Opens an email request that Introd verifies; nothing is deleted by clicking it |
| Request complete privacy assistance | Email [privacy@getintrod.ai](mailto:privacy@getintrod.ai) | Introd confirms the request, identity checks, and policy timeline |

These controls are separate. Clearing extension data does not disconnect a provider or close an Introd account. The extension has no pause button; see [Sync and consent](/chrome-extension/linkedin-integration) for what stops it.
