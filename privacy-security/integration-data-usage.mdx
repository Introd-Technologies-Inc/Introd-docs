Connect only the sources you are authorized to use. Each source adds a different kind of relationship context.

> Last reviewed against current extension, dashboard, and API source on October 30, 2026.

## LinkedIn

The Introd Chrome extension currently supports:

- linking to your Introd account from your signed-in dashboard
- syncing your first-degree connections after you approve a consent prompt
- a small **i.** button on supported LinkedIn profiles, people-search results, and company People pages that looks up Introd paths for that profile

The extension does not ask for your LinkedIn password. It does not send connection requests, messages, likes, follows, or introductions. It does not capture profile data as you browse.

During an approved sync it can send, for you and each first-degree connection: name, LinkedIn profile address and public identifier, headline, photo reference, current title and company when LinkedIn gives them as a structured position, location, the date you connected, and education. When Introd asks, it can also read more public profile and company details for people in your first-degree network. LinkedIn visibility, page rendering, session state, and rate limits can make a sync partial.

The **i.** button sends only the profile's address and public identifier, only when you choose it, as a read-only lookup.

Your sync approval is stored in the browser, tied to your Introd user and workspace. **Revoke sync permission** and **Enable extension** off stop new syncs; neither deletes data already in Introd. See [Sync and consent](/chrome-extension/linkedin-integration) for every control.

You can also bring LinkedIn connections in with a `Connections.csv` export from LinkedIn. That upload needs a consent checkbox and is used for relationship path discovery.

## Google Workspace

The current Google sync requests access to:

- Gmail metadata, including available From, To, Cc, Bcc, Date, and Subject headers
- read-only Calendar event metadata
- read-only Contacts data
- basic account identity needed to connect the correct provider account

The current sync does not import Gmail message bodies, meeting contents, or private notes.

## Microsoft 365

The current Microsoft sync requests access to:

- basic Outlook mail metadata exposed through Mail.ReadBasic
- read-only Outlook Calendar metadata
- basic account identity needed to connect the correct provider account

The current sync is configured for metadata-only mail processing. It does not import Outlook message bodies, meeting contents, or private notes.

## How Introd uses this data

Authorized provider data can contribute to:

- searchable people and company records
- available connector and path context
- relationship evidence counts and sync status
- relationship-strength and path-confidence signals shown by the product

Those signals rank available evidence. They are not guarantees that a relationship is current, that a connector will agree, or that an introduction will succeed.

## Disconnect behavior

For Google Workspace and Microsoft 365:

1. Open [Integrations](https://app.getintrod.ai/settings/integrations).
2. Open the connected provider card.
3. Choose **Disconnect** and confirm.

**Expected result:** Introd clears its stored connection and stops future sync jobs. Previously imported Introd records remain. Introd asks Google to revoke its grant; for Microsoft, also remove Introd in your Microsoft account's app permissions.

Revoking provider-side consent and deleting imported data are separate actions. See [Deletion and Privacy Requests](/privacy-security/gdpr-and-deletion).

## Sync failures

| What you see | Meaning or recovery |
| --- | --- |
| Partial | Some provider surfaces are connected or some work remains; open the provider details |
| Reauthorization needed | Complete provider consent again from Integrations |
| Zero new evidence | Confirm the connected account contains eligible metadata, then retry once |
| LinkedIn needs attention | Sign in to the LinkedIn profile you linked, then retry from the popup |
| LinkedIn sync needs attention | Open the popup and read the issue; the extension also retries automatically |

For the exact provider scopes, see [OAuth Permissions](/privacy-security/oauth-permissions).
