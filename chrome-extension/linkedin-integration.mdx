> Last reviewed against extension version 3.1.24 on October 30, 2026.

The extension has two separate LinkedIn behaviors. Neither sends messages, likes, follows, or connection requests.

## Prerequisites

- The extension is installed and [linked to your Introd account](/chrome-extension/installation).
- LinkedIn is signed in in the same Chrome profile.

## 1. First-degree connection sync (needs your consent)

Before the first sync, the popup asks **Allow first-degree connection sync?** It says that Introd will open a background LinkedIn connections tab and sync only your first-degree connection identities to this account and workspace. It lists three points:

- Only your direct, first-degree connections
- No messages, likes, follows, or connection requests
- You can close this popup while sync continues

Choose **Allow direct-connection sync** to approve, or **Not now** to decline. Nothing syncs until you approve.

Your approval is saved in this browser and is tied to your exact Introd user and workspace. A different account or workspace, or an explicit sign-out, removes it. If your session expires and you link the same account and workspace again, you are not asked again.

### How a sync runs

Introd's service decides whether there is work to do. After you approve, the extension checks in with Introd from time to time in the background. When Introd asks for work, the extension reads your first-degree connections from LinkedIn in a signed-in LinkedIn tab. If you have no LinkedIn tab open, it opens an inactive one at your connections page and closes it when the run ends. It does not close tabs you opened yourself.

This means a sync can continue or resume without the popup open, and without you starting it again. Closing the popup does not stop it. If the background tab closes, the run retries later.

### What syncs

For you and for each first-degree connection, the extension can send:

- name, LinkedIn profile address and public identifier, headline, and profile photo reference
- current title and company, when LinkedIn gives them as a structured current position
- location and the date you connected
- education, when available

When Introd asks, the extension can also read more public profile and company details for people in your first-degree network, such as positions, education, and company page fields. The extension does not send your LinkedIn credentials. It does not read your messages, your feed, or second- and third-degree connections.

Avatar source addresses are redacted from the extension's stored diagnostics.

## 2. The on-page **i.** button

On supported profiles, people-search results, and company People pages, the extension can show a small **i.** button next to the profile. Choosing it opens **Review a potential warm path**.

- It sends only the profile's address and public identifier to Introd, and only when you choose it.
- It is a read-only lookup. It does not create people or relationships.
- It can show a connector, strength, and evidence, or a message such as "This profile is not mapped by a permissioned source yet" or "No evidence-backed route is available for this profile yet."
- It is not LinkedIn verification, an endorsement, or a guarantee of willingness.

The button appears only if Introd has authorized it for your account and you have **Show Introd on LinkedIn profiles** on. If Introd has not authorized it for your account, the setting is disabled and reads "Not available for this linked account yet."

The extension does not capture profile data as you browse. **Profile auto-capture is off** and cannot be turned on from the settings.

## Sync states

| Visible state | Meaning | Next action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn is ready to sync | No sync has run yet | Choose **Start sync** |
| Your LinkedIn network is syncing… | A sync is in progress | Wait. You can close the popup |
| LinkedIn network up to date | Introd reports the sync is current | None |
| LinkedIn sync partially complete | Some connections are saved and more remain | Choose **Retry**, or wait for the automatic retry |
| LinkedIn sync needs attention | The last run failed | Read the message and choose **Retry** |
| LinkedIn needs attention | The signed-in LinkedIn profile does not match, or you need to reconnect | Sign in to the linked LinkedIn profile |
| LinkedIn sync paused | Introd reports the sync as paused | Choose **Sync now** |
| LinkedIn sync unavailable | Sync is turned off for this account | Choose **Review** |

## Stop or limit the extension

The popup has no pause or stop button for a running sync.

| Control | Where | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Enable extension** off | Options page | Turns off LinkedIn pages, the **i.** button, sync, background work, and Introd requests. The popup shows "GetIntrod is paused" |
| **Revoke sync permission** | Options page | Removes your sync approval and local sync state. No new sync runs until you approve again. It does not delete data already in Introd |
| **Sign out** | Popup settings | Unlinks this browser and clears account-scoped local data and your approval |
| **Show Introd on LinkedIn profiles** off | Popup settings | Hides the **i.** button. Sync is unaffected |
| **Clear local data** | Options page, Danger zone | Clears this browser's extension data and signs you out. Introd's stored data is not deleted |
| Disable the extension | `chrome://extensions` | Stops everything the extension does |

To open the **Options page**, right-click the extension's toolbar icon and choose **Options**, or open `chrome://extensions`, choose **Details** for Introd for LinkedIn, then **Extension options**.

Other popup privacy settings: **Only analyze supported pages** is on by default. **Allow contextual page detection** is off by default and, when on, lets the popup read the current tab's address only to offer an **Ask Introd** link for a supported profile.

<Warning>
  Stopping the extension does not remove connections already synced to Introd. To remove data, see [Deletion and privacy requests](/privacy-security/gdpr-and-deletion).
</Warning>

## Limitations

- LinkedIn renders large networks incrementally, so imported totals can remain below the visible total.
- A partial sync is not a completed sync, even though saved records remain available.
- Missing role, company, photo, or location data can produce an incomplete contact.
- A synced target can still have no verified warm path.
- LinkedIn interface changes can interrupt collection until the extension is updated.

## Verify in Introd

Open [Network Setup](https://app.getintrod.ai/setup-network) or [Integrations](https://app.getintrod.ai/settings/integrations). Confirm the LinkedIn status, imported count, and last-sync time before relying on the data.
