**Introd for LinkedIn** does three things. It never messages anyone on your behalf.

- **Links to your Introd account** from your signed-in dashboard. It never asks for your LinkedIn password.
- **Syncs your first-degree LinkedIn connections** to your Introd workspace, only after you approve a consent prompt.
- **Shows path context on LinkedIn.** On supported profiles, people-search results, and company People pages it can show a small **i.** button that looks up Introd paths for that profile.

> Last reviewed against extension version 3.1.24 on October 30, 2026. The manifest and controls described here were also checked in the published 3.1.24 package.

## Prerequisites

- Desktop Chrome
- An approved Introd account
- A LinkedIn account signed in in the same Chrome profile, for LinkedIn sync

## What the extension does not do

- It does not send LinkedIn messages, likes, follows, or connection requests.
- It does not send an introduction request or email.
- It does not capture profile data as you browse. The **i.** button sends only the profile's address to Introd, and only when you click it.
- It does not ask for your LinkedIn password.
- It does not read other websites. Its content scripts run only on the LinkedIn and Introd pages listed in [Permissions](/chrome-extension/permissions).
- It does not guarantee complete coverage of your LinkedIn connections.

## How to stop it

There is **no pause button** in the popup. Closing the popup does not stop a sync. Use one of these, from least to most complete:

| Control | Where | What it stops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Enable extension** off | Options page (right-click the toolbar icon, then **Options**) | LinkedIn pages, the **i.** button, sync, background work, and Introd requests |
| **Revoke sync permission** | Options page | Future syncs, until you approve the consent prompt again. Clears local sync state |
| **Show Introd on LinkedIn profiles** off | Popup settings | Hides the **i.** button only. Does not affect sync |
| **Sign out** | Popup settings | Unlinks this browser from your Introd account and clears its local data |
| Disable the extension | `chrome://extensions` | Everything the extension does |

**Clear local data** removes the extension's saved data in this browser and signs you out. It does not delete anything Introd already stored. See [Deletion and privacy requests](/privacy-security/gdpr-and-deletion).

## Basic workflow

1. [Install the extension](/chrome-extension/installation).
2. Open the popup and choose **Connect from Introd dashboard**.
3. Finish sign-in in the Introd tab that opens, then return to the popup.
4. Keep LinkedIn signed in in the same Chrome profile.
5. Choose **Sync now** (or **Start sync**) and answer the consent prompt.
6. Verify the imported count and status in the popup and in Introd.
7. Open a supported LinkedIn page to see path context.
