## Common issues

| What you see | Meaning | Recovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GetIntrod is unavailable. The extension could not reach Introd. | The popup could not load | Choose **Retry**. If it continues, reload the extension in `chrome://extensions` |
| Link this extension from your signed-in Introd dashboard | The extension is not linked to an account | Choose **Connect from Introd dashboard** |
| Finish sign in | The Introd tab has not finished linking | Finish sign-in in that tab, then choose **I finished signing in** |
| Allow first-degree connection sync? | Sync needs your approval | Choose **Allow direct-connection sync**, or **Not now** to decline |
| Consent required | Your approval is missing for this account or workspace | Choose **Sync now** and answer the prompt |
| GetIntrod is paused | **Enable extension** is off | Choose **Enable GetIntrod** |
| LinkedIn needs attention | The signed-in LinkedIn profile does not match, or LinkedIn needs a new sign-in | Sign in to the LinkedIn profile you linked, then retry |
| LinkedIn sync partially complete | Some connections are saved and more remain | Choose **Retry**. The extension also retries automatically |
| LinkedIn sync needs attention | The run failed | Read the message, refresh LinkedIn, and choose **Retry** |
| LinkedIn status unavailable | Introd could not report the status. Your saved data is unchanged | Choose **Retry** |
| Could not open a background LinkedIn tab for sync | Chrome blocked the inactive tab | Open LinkedIn yourself, sign in, and retry |
| Timed out waiting for LinkedIn to load | LinkedIn did not load in time | Check your connection and retry |
| Not available for this linked account yet | Introd has not authorized the **i.** button for your account | No action. This does not affect sync |
| No path context on a profile | The page is unsupported, evidence is missing, or sync is incomplete | Open a LinkedIn profile, complete a sync, and refresh |

## Verify the browser profile

Introd and LinkedIn must be signed in in the same Chrome profile that has the extension. A private window or another Chrome profile has separate sign-ins and extension state.

## Stop a sync

The popup has no pause or stop button, and closing the popup or the background LinkedIn tab does not reliably stop a sync. To stop it:

1. Open the extension's **Options page**: right-click the toolbar icon and choose **Options**.
2. Turn **Enable extension** off, or choose **Revoke sync permission**.

To stop everything, disable the extension in `chrome://extensions`. See [Sync and consent](/chrome-extension/linkedin-integration).

## Reset browser-local extension data

Use this only when normal retry and sign-in recovery fail:

1. Open the **Options page**.
2. In **Danger zone**, choose **Clear local data** and confirm.
3. Link the extension to your account again and answer the consent prompt.

This clears the extension's data in this browser and signs you out. It does not delete anything Introd has stored.

## Before requesting help

Collect:

- the extension version from its settings
- your Chrome version
- the exact visible status and error text
- whether LinkedIn and Introd are signed in in the same Chrome profile
- the approximate time of the failure

Email those details to [help@getintrod.ai](mailto:help@getintrod.ai). Never include a password, OAuth code, access token, browser cookie, or extension package.
