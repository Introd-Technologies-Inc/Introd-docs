## The shortest successful path

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request access and sign in">
    Open [app.getintrod.ai](https://app.getintrod.ai). Introd asks new users to **Request access** and joins you to a waitlist until your access is approved. Introd emails you when it is. See [Request access](/getting-started/create-account).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan and finish required setup">
    Pick a plan on **Choose your plan**, then connect and verify your first network source. Until the first source is verified, the app keeps you in setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search a real target">
    Use [Search people](/people/search-people), then open a result that includes enough relationship context to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review before you ask">
    Check the connector, evidence, and uncertainty. If the path is weak or incomplete, gather more context instead of treating it as a warm introduction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a connector review">
    Use [Prepare an introduction](/introductions/create-request). Saving a draft contacts no one. Starting a connector review asks the connector, not the target, to review the request.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What these docs promise

- Instructions describe behavior reviewed against the current product source, not future product ideas.
- Weak or missing relationship evidence is labeled as a limitation, not converted into a confident path.
- Introd never contacts the person you want to meet automatically. A person reviews and intentionally acts at each step.
- Internal architecture, deployment, credentials, and operator runbooks are not part of the public documentation.

If a guide does not match what you see, contact [help@getintrod.ai](mailto:help@getintrod.ai) and include the page URL and the step that differed.
