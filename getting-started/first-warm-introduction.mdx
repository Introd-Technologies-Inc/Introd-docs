---

This guide covers the supported introduction workflow from a reviewed path.

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in to Introd.
- At least one real contact is visible in [People](https://app.getintrod.ai/search/people).
- The target has an available connector or path that you can review.
- You know why the introduction is relevant and appropriate.

## 1. Find the target

1. Open [People](https://app.getintrod.ai/search/people).
2. Search by the target's name, title, or company.
3. Select the target row to open its side panel.

If no result appears, verify your imported counts in [Network Setup](https://app.getintrod.ai/setup-network). Do not create a target from guessed identity data.

## 2. Review the path

1. In the side panel, choose **Review path before requesting**.
2. On **Potential Intro Path**, review the connector, the route evidence under **Why this path works**, **Path Strength**, and **Path confidence**.
3. Confirm that names, roles, and companies match the people you intend to involve.

If Introd shows no path, weak evidence, or reports that evidence is still syncing, stop this attempt. Choose another target or add more authorized relationship data.

## 3. Prepare the introduction

1. Choose **Request intro** for the connector you reviewed, or **Save path** to come back later. The path page notes that the request can be tracked in Introductions.
2. Confirm the target and connector at the top.
3. Edit the message so the connector can understand the relevance and decline comfortably. The limit is 1,000 characters.
4. Remove any context the connector is not allowed to forward.

## 4. Save the draft or start the connector review

On **Draft & Review**:

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Save draft** | Stores the draft in Introd. Introd reports **Draft saved**. Nobody is contacted. |
| **Send for review** or **Create review link** | Starts a connector review. The button label depends on your account. See below. |

The label tells you which path your account uses:

- **Send for review** appears when you have a verified Google or Microsoft sender with sending turned on. Introd then tries to deliver the connector's review request from that account. The status banner shows only provider-confirmed progress.
- **Create review link** appears when you have no verified sender. Introd creates a private, expiring link and tells you to share it with the connector yourself. Introd sends no message in this case.

In both cases the review is addressed to the **connector**. The person you want to meet is not contacted automatically. They are reached only after the connector accepts, and the introduction shows the next step.

<Warning>
  A saved draft and a created review link do not message anyone. A connector review is not an introduction. Mark progress in Introd only after the real event has happened.
</Warning>

Open [Introductions](https://app.getintrod.ai/introductions) to review the saved record and its status.

## Draft checklist

- Target identity and company are correct.
- Connector identity is correct.
- The message explains why the conversation is relevant now.
- The connector has an easy way to decline.
- The message contains no confidential or unapproved information.

## Failure states

| What you see | Recovery |
| --- | --- |
| Could not load synced people | Confirm your Introd session and reload People |
| No path, or weak or incomplete evidence | Continue sync or choose another target |
| Provider delivery status is unavailable | Introd does not assume the message was delivered; check your sent mail or share the review link yourself |
| Retry provider delivery | Review the account and try again |
| Draft not visible in Introductions | Clear filters and search by target or connector name |

## Current limitations

- Path Strength and Path confidence are ranking signals, not outcome probabilities.
- Shared community or company context is not proof that an introduction is appropriate.
- Priority, **Keep me updated**, and attachments appear on the draft screen but are not available yet.
