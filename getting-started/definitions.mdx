---
title: "Definitions"
description: "Definitions for the customer-facing relationship, sync, path, and introduction terms used in Introd."
---

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

## Synced contact

A person record imported from an authorized source or added to your Introd network. A synced contact can still have missing company, role, or relationship fields.

## Warm path

A route shown by Introd between you, a possible connector, and a target. A path is available only when the current graph returns supporting relationship evidence.

## Connector

The person positioned between you and the target in a proposed path. Connector ranking is a recommendation for review, not proof that the person will make an introduction.

## Path Strength

A value out of 100 shown on a path, labeled "Introd relationship estimate". It summarizes the evidence Introd holds for that route, and **Path confidence** is a separate value out of 100 for how strongly that evidence supports it. Treat it as a relative ranking aid within Introd, not as a published probability or guarantee. Introd does not publish a formula for it.

## Graph confidence

A value from 0 to 100 representing the confidence value returned with current graph metadata. A low or missing value means the route needs more review; it does not become reliable merely because a target exists.

## Evidence chip

A short label returned with graph data, such as shared community context, alumni overlap, connector reputation, or a recent relationship signal. Evidence chips explain a ranking input; they do not establish consent to make an introduction.

## Relationship labels

Contact-level relationship strength can appear as **Strong**, **Medium**, or **Weak**. A **Weak** band can read **Limited evidence** when a connected source has not been read or reconnected. Both are current product signals that can change as relationship data changes; neither is a promise about a person or outcome.

## Partial sync

A sync that saved imported records but did not reach the full total LinkedIn exposed to the extension. It can be resumed from the saved checkpoint.

## Introduction draft

A target, connector, and message saved with draft status. Saving a draft does not contact anyone.

## Introduction workflow status

The state used to organize an introduction record in Introd. A status change tracks workflow state; it is not, by itself, evidence that an email or LinkedIn message was delivered.

## Connector review

A private, expiring page where the connector accepts or declines your introduction request. Starting a connector review addresses the connector, not the person you want to meet.
