## The customer journey

| Step | Open | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request access | [Request access](https://app.getintrod.ai/sign-up) | Your request joins the waitlist; Introd emails you when it is approved |
| Choose a plan | **Choose your plan** | You pick Free or a paid plan |
| Pick your goal | **Getting Started** | You choose Fundraising, Customers, Hiring, or Partnerships |
| Connect a source | [Network Setup](https://app.getintrod.ai/setup-network) | The source reports a connected state or an imported count |
| Verify integrations | [Integrations](https://app.getintrod.ai/settings/integrations) | Connection and sync status are visible |
| Find a target | [People](https://app.getintrod.ai/search/people) | Synced people appear, or Introd reports that no matching data is available |
| Inspect paths | Select a person, then review the path | Introd displays connector evidence or an empty state |
| Start the introduction | **Request intro** or **Save draft** | A draft or connector review is recorded |
| Track it | [Introductions](https://app.getintrod.ai/introductions) | The record appears with its current status |

## Current boundaries

- Access is approved before use. New users join a waitlist first.
- Until your first source is verified, the app keeps you in setup. **Continue to dashboard** on Getting Started defers it.
- The Introd extension is available from the Chrome Web Store. It syncs your first-degree LinkedIn connections only after you approve a consent prompt.
- LinkedIn coverage depends on what LinkedIn exposes to your signed-in session. A partial sync is a valid state.
- Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 contribute relationship metadata through explicit OAuth consent. On the Free plan they can require a paid plan or connector add-on.
- A warm path appears only when the synced graph contains usable evidence. Introd does not guarantee a path for every target.
- Introd never contacts the person you want to meet automatically.
