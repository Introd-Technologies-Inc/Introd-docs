---
title: "Compare Warm Intro Paths"
description: "How to compare the connector routes Introd shows for a target."
---

Open a target's **Warm Intro Path** to compare the routes currently supported by your synced network evidence.

## What the view shows

| Area | How to use it |
| --- | --- |
| Path rail | Confirm the selected connector chain and target. |
| Path Strength | Use the `/100` value to compare routes shown for this target. |
| Why this path works | Read the reasons and evidence attached to the selected route. |
| Relationship context | Review the connector, target, mutual context, and suggested next step. |
| Other paths | Select another available route and compare its evidence. |

Introd orders available routes by the score shown in the path view. A higher position means that Introd ranked that route ahead of the other routes it returned for the target.

<Note>
  Use the ranking to compare options in the current view. Do not treat it as an objective grade for a person or as a universal score that is comparable across every target.
</Note>

## A practical review

1. Check the connector identity and role.
2. Read every visible reason under **Why this path works**.
3. Look for specific context you recognize, such as a named mutual connection or recorded path evidence.
4. Compare the alternatives instead of acting on rank alone.
5. Ask the connector whether they are comfortable making the introduction.

The **Estimated success** percentage is a directional estimate shown by the product. It is not a calibrated promise that an introduction, reply, meeting, or other outcome will happen.

## Empty and incomplete results

- **No alternate verified paths are available yet** means Introd did not return another supported route for this target.
- **No named mutual connectors available yet** means the current result has no named mutual connector to display.
- **Sync more relationship evidence** means the product needs more applicable data before it can explain or compare a route.
- A blank or unavailable score means there is no usable score to interpret yet.

Missing evidence is an unknown, not a negative judgment about the connector or target.

## Next step

Selecting **Request intro** opens the introduction request flow for the chosen connector. Review the message and recipient context yourself; the ranking does not provide permission to contact anyone.
