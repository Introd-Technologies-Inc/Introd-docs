## Communities

Open [Communities](https://app.getintrod.ai/communities) to browse communities, confirm an affiliation, review suggestions, and manage invitations. When you confirm an affiliation, Introd asks **Who can see this affiliation?** so you choose the audience for each one.

If no communities appear, there is no community option available to you yet. Do not interpret an empty list as evidence that a person is outside your real-world network.

## Workspace sharing

A collection shared with your workspace shows its people to teammates only while the collection's owner shares their network with the workspace.

The platform page that holds graph-sharing level settings is an Introd administrator tool. It is not available to customer accounts, so this documentation does not describe those settings as something you configure. If you need your sharing choices reviewed or changed, email [help@getintrod.ai](mailto:help@getintrod.ai) or [privacy@getintrod.ai](mailto:privacy@getintrod.ai).

<Warning>
  Treat sharing as a privacy choice. Confirm the intended audience before you share a collection or confirm a community affiliation. If you are unsure, keep it private.
</Warning>

## Sharing is not consent

Sharing or community membership controls how Introd may use relationship evidence in path discovery. It does not mean that:

- a connector agreed to make a specific introduction;
- a target agreed to be contacted;
- a ranked path is accurate in every detail; or
- an introduction will succeed.

Confirm the connector's willingness before requesting an introduction, regardless of rank or sharing.
