The **Relationship Intelligence** screen includes graph-sharing controls. The active level determines where Introd can use the relationship evidence described by that setting.

## Sharing levels shown in Introd

| Level | Scope described in the app |
| --- | --- |
| Private | No relationship evidence is shared. |
| Organization Only | Aggregate evidence can support warm paths inside approved workspaces. |
| Trusted Community | Aggregate evidence can support paths inside selected communities. |
| Public Warm Intros | Connector identity and aggregate strength can appear in intro paths. |

To change the setting, open **Relationship Intelligence**, review each description, and select the level you intend to use. The active option is marked in the interface.

<Warning>
  Increasing the sharing scope is a meaningful privacy choice. Confirm the intended audience before changing it. If you are uncertain, leave the setting at **Private** and contact your workspace administrator or Introd support.
</Warning>

## Trusted communities

The **Trusted Communities** section lists communities available to your account. Selected communities are included in the current community-sharing configuration.

If no communities appear, there is no community option available to select in that view. Do not interpret an empty list as evidence that a person is outside your real-world network.

## Sharing is not consent

A sharing level controls how Introd may use relationship evidence in path discovery. It does not mean that:

- a connector agreed to make a specific introduction;
- a target agreed to be contacted;
- a ranked path is accurate in every detail; or
- an introduction will succeed.

Confirm the connector's willingness before requesting an introduction, regardless of rank or sharing level.
