## How do I get an Introd account?

Open [Request access](https://app.getintrod.ai/sign-up). Introd approves access before you can use a workspace: you join a waitlist and Introd emails you when your access is approved. After that you choose a plan and connect your first network source. See [Request access](/getting-started/create-account).

## Where can I install the Chrome extension?

From the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/eoancpljacjbffdfjlbchdaeoainbkkf) or the install action in Network Setup. Introd has not published a separate Microsoft Edge listing. Do not install a copy from an advertisement, search result, or forwarded archive. See [Install the extension](/chrome-extension/installation).

## What does the extension do?

- It links to your Introd account from your signed-in dashboard.
- It syncs your first-degree LinkedIn connections, only after you approve a consent prompt.
- It shows a small **i.** button on supported LinkedIn profiles, people-search results, and company People pages that looks up Introd paths for that profile.

It does not send LinkedIn messages, likes, follows, connection requests, or introductions. It does not ask for your LinkedIn password and does not capture profile data as you browse. See [Sync and consent](/chrome-extension/linkedin-integration).

## How do I stop the extension syncing?

The popup has no pause button. Open the extension's **Options page** (right-click the toolbar icon, then **Options**) and turn **Enable extension** off, or choose **Revoke sync permission**. Disabling the extension in `chrome://extensions` stops everything it does. Closing the popup or the background LinkedIn tab does not reliably stop a sync. None of these delete data already in Introd.

## Why does Introd say LinkedIn needs attention?

The extension uses the LinkedIn session in the same Chrome profile, and it expects the signed-in LinkedIn profile to match the one you linked. Sign in to that profile, then choose **Retry** in the popup.

## Why is my LinkedIn sync partial?

LinkedIn visibility, page rendering, session state, and rate limits can leave some connections unprocessed. Open the popup to read its status. The extension also retries automatically. Compare the imported count in [Network Setup](https://app.getintrod.ai/setup-network).

## Why do I not see a warm path?

A target needs enough imported relationship context and an available connector for Introd to display a path.

1. Confirm that Network Setup shows imported connections.
2. Confirm Google or Microsoft sync status if you connected either source.
3. Search for the target again in [People](https://app.getintrod.ai/search/people).
4. If the path view still shows no path or only weak evidence, choose another target or add more authorized relationship data.

Shared company or community context is not proof that a warm introduction exists.

## How are path scores calculated?

The product shows relationship bands (**Strong**, **Medium**, **Weak**), **Path Strength** and **Path confidence** values out of 100 on a path. Introd does not publish a customer-facing formula. Treat the values as relative ranking signals, not probabilities or guarantees.

## What do Google and Microsoft import?

Google Workspace uses Gmail header metadata, Calendar event metadata, and Contacts data. Microsoft 365 uses basic Outlook mail metadata and Calendar metadata. The current syncs do not import email bodies, meeting contents, or private notes. On the Free plan, these connections can require a paid plan or a connector add-on.

See [Integration Data Usage](/privacy-security/integration-data-usage) and [OAuth Permissions](/privacy-security/oauth-permissions).

## Does Save draft send the introduction?

No. **Save draft** stores the draft in Introd and contacts no one. **Create review link** makes a private link for the connector that you share yourself. **Send for review** can deliver the connector's review request from your own connected account when your account is set up to send. Introd never contacts the person you want to meet automatically. See [Introductions](/introductions).

## How do I disconnect Google or Microsoft?

Open [Integrations](https://app.getintrod.ai/settings/integrations), open the provider card, and choose **Disconnect**. Future sync stops. Previously imported Introd records remain. Introd asks Google to revoke its grant; for Microsoft, also remove Introd in your Microsoft account's app permissions.

## Does clearing extension data delete my Introd data?

No. **Clear local data** removes the extension's saved data in that Chrome installation and signs you out. It does not delete server-side Introd records, disconnect Google or Microsoft, or close the account.

## How do I close my account or request deletion?

In [Settings](https://app.getintrod.ai/settings), choose **Request verified closure**. It opens an email request that Introd verifies. There is no button that deletes your account on its own. For access, correction, or deletion of your data, email [privacy@getintrod.ai](mailto:privacy@getintrod.ai). See [Deletion and Privacy Requests](/privacy-security/gdpr-and-deletion).
