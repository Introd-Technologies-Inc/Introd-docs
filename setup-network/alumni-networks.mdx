The current Alumni and VC Communities page stores community selections on your Introd profile.

[Open Alumni and VC Communities](https://app.getintrod.ai/setup-network/alumni)

## Save a selection

1. Find a community in the fixed catalog.
2. Select **Join**.
3. Wait for the button to change to **Joined**.

Select **Joined** again to remove that saved selection.

<Warning>
This action does not verify your affiliation, join an external alumni or VC organization, import that organization's members, or notify anyone. It records only your selection inside Introd.
</Warning>

The current connections table shows your saved community entries. Do not interpret it as verified network overlap with other members.
