The standalone Alumni and VC Communities setup step has been replaced by **Communities**. The old address now opens [Communities](https://app.getintrod.ai/communities).

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

## Where alumni and VC networks are now

In Communities, alumni networks are a community type (**School**) and an **Alumni network** category. Accelerator, VC ecosystem, founder, and professional communities sit alongside them.

- **Confirm your affiliation** records your relationship to a community and asks who can see it.
- **Browse** and **Recommended** tabs help you find communities. **My communities** and **Invitations** show yours.
- **Create a community** lets you start your own. Custom communities begin unlisted and are visible only through a private link.

<Warning>
  Confirming an affiliation records it inside Introd. It does not join an outside organization, import its members, or notify anyone. Introd shows suggested communities privately until you review them.
</Warning>
