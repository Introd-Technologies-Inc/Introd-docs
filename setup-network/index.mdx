Network Setup is the control center for bringing relationship data into Introd.

[Open Network Setup](https://app.getintrod.ai/setup-network)

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

## Recommended setup path

1. Connect LinkedIn through the Introd Chrome extension and approve its sync consent prompt.
2. Connect Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 if you want email and calendar relationship evidence. On the Free plan these can require a paid plan or connector add-on.
3. Import a CSV only for contacts not covered by a connected source.
4. Open **People** and verify that real records appear.
5. Review connection status and the last-updated time before relying on paths.

Until your first source is verified, the app keeps you in setup. **Continue to dashboard** on Getting Started defers it.

## Sources

| Source | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Direct connections and relationship paths, through the Chrome extension |
| Google Workspace | Contacts, Gmail, and Calendar context |
| Microsoft 365 | Outlook mail and Calendar context |
| Apple Contacts | Export your contacts and import the file; there is no direct connection |
| CSV | Preview and import a contact spreadsheet |

## Setup sections

The setup rail shows **Sources**, **CSV import**, **People**, and **Team**. CSV import, People, and Team are marked **Manage** (optional). Alumni and group affiliations moved to [Communities](https://app.getintrod.ai/communities).

<Note>
The setup progress indicator is an orientation aid. Confirm the provider status, contact count, and actual People results before treating setup as complete.
</Note>
