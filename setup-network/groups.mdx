The standalone Groups setup step has been replaced by **Communities**. The old address now opens [Communities](https://app.getintrod.ai/communities).

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

## What to use instead

Use **Communities** to find, confirm, and manage the networks you belong to. Community invitations are email invitations to a private link:

- You can invite up to 10 people at once.
- Introd says an invitation was sent only when the email provider accepted it. Otherwise it says "A new private link is ready. It has not been emailed." and you can share the link yourself.

<Warning>
  Joining or confirming a community does not verify membership elsewhere, create a shared workspace, or message anyone on your behalf.
</Warning>
