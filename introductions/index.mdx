## Four different things

The word "send" can mean four different things. Introd keeps them apart:

| Step | What it means | Contacts anyone? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Save draft** | Introd stores your message and context | No |
| **Create review link** | Introd makes a private, expiring link for the connector to review. You share it yourself | Only if you share the link |
| **Send for review** | Introd tries to deliver the connector's review request from your own verified Google or Microsoft account and shows provider-confirmed status | The connector, when your account is set up to send |
| The introduction itself | The connector introduces you to the target | Done by the connector, not by Introd |

Introd never contacts the person you want to meet automatically. The target is reached only after the connector accepts, through a separate private review. Introd's wording is explicit: "Introd sends nothing to them automatically."

<Warning>
  Which button you see, **Send for review** or **Create review link**, depends on whether you have a verified sender with sending turned on in your connected accounts. Introd shows delivery as sent only when the email provider confirms it. If status is unavailable, Introd does not infer delivery.
</Warning>

## Core workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the path">
    Confirm the target, connector, and evidence in [View and verify paths](/people/view-paths).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the request">
    Edit the message, remove unverified claims, and save a draft if it needs more work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connector review">
    Choose **Send for review** or **Create review link**. The connector opens a private page and accepts or declines.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintain the status">
    Use the list to keep the stored state aligned with what actually happened.
  </Step>
</Steps>
