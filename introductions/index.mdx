Use **Introductions** to store the target, connector, request context, and current workflow state for a warm introduction.

<Card title="Open Introductions" icon="arrow-up-right" href="https://app.getintrod.ai/introductions">
  Sign-in is required. The page shows introduction records available to your workspace.
</Card>

## Two separate responsibilities

| Introd records | A person remains responsible for |
| --- | --- |
| Target and connector context | Confirming the identities and relationship |
| Draft subject and message | Reviewing every claim and edit |
| Pending, active, or closed workflow state | Contacting the connector through an approved channel |
| Stored progress and scheduled date | Confirming whether an external message was actually delivered |

<Warning>
  Creating, submitting, scheduling, or moving an introduction record does not deliver an email or LinkedIn message. Introd tracks the workflow; external outreach remains a human action.
</Warning>

## Core workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the path">
    Confirm the target, connector, and evidence in [View and verify paths](/people/view-paths).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare the request">
    Edit the subject and message, remove unverified claims, and save a draft when it needs more work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the tracked request">
    When ready, move the record into the pending workflow. Complete any external outreach yourself.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Maintain the status">
    Use the pipeline or list to keep the stored state aligned with what actually happened.
  </Step>
</Steps>
