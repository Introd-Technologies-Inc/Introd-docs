---

After reviewing a person and path, continue to the introduction flow with the target and connector already chosen.

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

## Before you start

Confirm:

- the target is the correct person;
- the proposed connector is someone you can appropriately ask;
- the path explanation matches the relationship you understand;
- the request has a specific, relevant purpose; and
- weak or missing evidence has not been treated as certainty.

## From the path

1. On **Potential Intro Path**, review the **Trusted relationship path** and the route evidence.
2. Choose **Save path** to keep it, or **Request intro** to continue.
3. On **Draft & Review**, edit the message.
4. Use **Save draft** while the request still needs review.
5. Choose **Send for review** or **Create review link** when you are ready for the connector to review it.

<Warning>
  These actions address the **connector**, not the person you want to meet. Introd does not contact the target automatically. See [Introductions](/introductions) for what each button does.
</Warning>

The target, connector, message, and available evidence are stored with the Introd request. Review the record on the [Introductions](/introductions) page.

If the selected route is not credible, close it and return to [View and verify paths](/people/view-paths).
