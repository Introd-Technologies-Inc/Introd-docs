---
title: "Review a Person"
description: "Use the person detail panel to validate identity, source, relationship context, and available next steps."
---

Select a row on [People](https://app.getintrod.ai/search/people) to open its detail panel.

## Validate the record

Review the fields that are actually present:

- name, role, company, and location;
- record source and LinkedIn profile link;
- stored relationship strength;
- available warm-path and mutual-connection counts;
- suggested connectors; and
- recent stored activity.

An empty field is unknown. Profile text from a sync, import, or public signal can be incomplete or stale, so verify important facts before using them in an introduction request.

## Choose the next action

From the detail panel you can:

- open all available paths;
- request an introduction through a selected connector;
- save an eligible contact to a collection; or
- open the source profile when a trusted LinkedIn URL is available.

Some actions are unavailable for unsaved public signals or incomplete records. Save or validate the person first rather than bypassing the disabled state.

<Tip>
  Use the panel to verify the person, then use [View and verify paths](/people/view-paths) to evaluate the route. Identity confidence and path confidence are separate decisions.
</Tip>
