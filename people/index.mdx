The **People** page brings together contacts and relationship records currently available to your workspace.

<Card title="Open People" icon="arrow-up-right" href="https://app.getintrod.ai/search/people">
  Sign-in is required. Search results depend on the sources that have finished syncing or importing.
</Card>

## What you can do

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Search people" icon="search" href="/people/search-people">
    Search the current network data by a person's name or other available profile text.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review paths" icon="route" href="/people/view-paths">
    Inspect available connectors and supporting context before acting.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Review a person" icon="contact" href="/people/relationship-insights">
    Understand the selected person's source, profile context, and path summary.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prepare a request" icon="handshake" href="/people/request-introductions">
    Carry a reviewed target and connector into an introduction draft.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Recommended workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search a real target">
    Start with a name, company, or role that is likely to appear in your current network data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open one result">
    Review the person's identity, source, available profile details, relationship label, and warm-path count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect the path">
    Use **View Paths** to confirm who the connector is and what evidence is available. Missing context remains missing; do not infer it from a score.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a human-reviewed next step">
    Save useful context, improve the relationship first, or prepare an introduction draft. Introd does not establish a connector's consent on your behalf.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Search results and path labels describe the data available to the workspace. They do not guarantee identity, willingness to connect, or a successful introduction.
</Warning>
