Collections organize people and companies around a goal without contacting anyone.

[Open Collections](https://app.getintrod.ai/collections)

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

## Create a collection

1. Choose **Create a new collection**, or pick a template (Investors, Industry, Events, Hiring, or Advisors).
2. Enter a name. A description is optional.
3. Choose **Private** or **Workspace** for visibility.
4. Create the collection.

**Private** means only you can see it. **Workspace** shares it with your team. A workspace collection is read-only to teammates, and its people are shown to them only while you share your network with your workspace. Confirm your sharing choices before using **Workspace** for sensitive lists.

You can also start a collection by choosing **Import from LinkedIn** (a `Connections.csv` upload) or **Import from CSV**.

## Add a person

1. Open **People** in the Introd sidebar.
2. Search for and select a saved contact.
3. In the person panel, choose the add-to-collection action and pick a collection.

If a result is only a public signal and is not yet a saved contact, Introd asks you to save it first.

## Review and maintain collections

Use search (Ctrl or Cmd + K), the sort menu, and the summary cards (total collections, people memberships, company memberships, and network warm paths) to find a collection. Open one to see its tabs: **People**, **Companies**, **Insights**, **Related**, and **Activity**. Teammates who do not own a collection see only **People** and **Companies**.

Each member can have a short note. You can remove a person when they no longer belong.

From a collection, **Take action** lets you create a mission, request introductions, or add the collection to a community. Requesting introductions opens the normal [introduction flow](/introductions); nothing is sent from the collection itself.
