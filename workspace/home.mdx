The **Dashboard** is the first item in the sidebar. It summarizes the records available in your workspace. If a section is empty, that data is not available yet.

[Open the Dashboard](https://app.getintrod.ai/)

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

## Start from the top

- **Goal shortcuts** and **Start a mission** begin a [mission](/workspace/missions).
- **Search your network** lets you choose **Person you need to meet** or **Company you need to reach** and search by name, or by company name or domain. It asks you to connect a network source first if you have none.
- Setup chips show the state of your **LinkedIn network**, **Email context**, and **Calendar context**.

If your network is not synced, connect a source in [Network Setup](/setup-network).

## What the Dashboard shows

- **Active Missions**, **People to meet**, and **Network growth** metrics. Mission data shows "Not enabled for this workspace" where missions are not enabled.
- **Your Active Missions**
- **People you should meet**, chosen from your network for the reasons shown. Introd does not contact anyone.
- **Your strongest paths**, with a score that is not the chance an introduction happens
- **Recent introductions**
- **Network Updates**
- **Upcoming actions**, which you can view, add to your calendar, or mark complete

Counts come from your workspace records and connected-source status. They are not sample data.

<Note>
Opening a contact, path, or the introduction flow does not send an introduction. Review the target, connector, and draft before taking any action outside Introd.
</Note>
