Home summarizes the records currently available in your Introd workspace. If a section is empty, that data is not available yet.

[Open Introd home](https://app.getintrod.ai/)

## Start with network readiness

The **Network readiness** panel shows whether contacts are available, when LinkedIn last synced, and counts for recent relationship changes and represented companies.

If network data is not synced:

1. Select **Sync network**.
2. Connect a supported source or import contacts.
3. Return to Home after the sync or import finishes.

## What Home shows

- **New warm paths**, **High trust connectors**, and **Strong company paths** when relationship evidence is available
- **Synced contacts** from your contacts table
- **Intro opportunities** and completed introduction records
- your strongest synced contacts and represented companies
- active introduction records and their current status

Counts come from your workspace records and connected-source status. They are not sample data.

## Choose the next task

- Select a contact to open its profile.
- Open **Search people** or **Search companies** to investigate a target.
- Open **View all** under Active introductions to review introduction records.
- Use **Create an intro request** to start a draft in the composer.

<Note>
Opening a contact, path, or composer does not send an introduction. Review the target, connector, and draft before taking any action outside Introd.
</Note>
