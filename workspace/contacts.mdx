The current primary contact experience is **People**. It searches saved contacts and, when available, clearly labeled public signals.

[Open People](https://app.getintrod.ai/search/people)

## Find a saved contact

1. Open **People** in the sidebar.
2. Search by name, title, company, skill, or another available attribute.
3. Submit the search and review the returned rows.
4. Select a row to open its detail panel.

The panel can show contact details, relationship evidence, connector counts, and links to paths when those fields exist. Missing fields remain unavailable rather than being filled with sample values.

## Add or import contacts

- Select **Add contact** to create one contact manually.
- Select **Import contacts** to use CSV, Google, or Outlook setup.
- Save a public-signal result before adding it to a collection.

## Contact actions

From a saved contact you can open the full profile, add a note, add the person to a collection, or review paths.

The profile **Message** action opens your email application when the contact has an email address. It does not send a message from Introd.

Some profile controls are visible before their workflows are available. Use only controls that open a form, link, or confirmation you can review.
