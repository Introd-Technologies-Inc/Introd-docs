**People** is the primary contact experience. It searches saved contacts and, when available, clearly labeled public signals.

[Open People](https://app.getintrod.ai/search/people)

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

## Find a saved contact

1. Open **People** in the sidebar.
2. Search with **Search people…** by name, title, company, or another attribute.
3. Choose a view: **All loaded**, **LinkedIn**, **Potential paths**, or **Needs enrichment**.
4. Select a row to open its side panel.

Sort by **Most relevant** or **Relationship strength**. Filters include community, location, tag, relationship strength (**Strong**, **Medium**, **Weak**), company, title, cohort, source (**LinkedIn**, **Manual**, or **Public signal**), connection degree (1st, 2nd, 3rd), potential path count, mutual connections, and recently added.

The side panel can show **Path confidence**, **Last interaction**, **Relationship source**, and **Profile status** (**Core profile complete** or **Needs enrichment**), plus links to paths when those fields exist. Missing fields remain unavailable rather than being filled with sample values. **Needs enrichment** marks an incomplete profile; the app does not offer an enrichment action there.

While 2nd- and 3rd-degree people are being computed, Introd shows "Calculating your extended network."

## Add or import contacts

- Choose **Add contact** to create one contact manually (full name is required).
- Choose **Import** to import a CSV. See [Import contacts](/setup-network/import-contacts).
- Save a public-signal result before adding it to a collection.

## Open a profile

A person's profile has tabs for **Overview**, **Relationship**, **Paths**, **Missions**, **Introductions**, and **Notes**. From it you can request an introduction link, add the person to a mission or collection, copy the profile link, and log re-engagement. A mail link appears only when the contact has an email address and opens your email application; it does not send a message from Introd.
