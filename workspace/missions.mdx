Missions turn a goal, such as raising money, hiring, or winning customers, into a tracked workspace item with matched people and paths. Missions never contact anyone for you.

[Open Missions](https://app.getintrod.ai/missions)

> Last reviewed against current product source on October 30, 2026.

<Note>
  Mission matching and Autopilot are enabled per workspace. If they are not on for yours, the app says "Missions are not enabled for this workspace yet." Contact [help@getintrod.ai](mailto:help@getintrod.ai) to ask about access.
</Note>

## Create a mission

1. Choose **New Mission**.
2. Work through the steps: **Goal**, **Scope**, **Targets**, and **Review**.
3. Choose a mission type: Fundraising, Sales, Hiring, Partnerships, Career growth, Community, or Custom.
4. Enter the outcome you want and optional scope such as geography, sector, stage, or size, and search for target companies or investors.
5. Create the mission. The app shows "Mission created. Matching has started."

A quick **Create a mission** dialog offers **Structure with Autopilot** and **Start matching**. Drafts in the wizard are saved on your device.

<Warning>
  Creating a mission does not send an introduction request, email a connector, or contact a target. Autopilot never sends an introduction on its own.
</Warning>

## Read the mission list

Filter by **All Missions**, **Active**, **Planning**, **Completed**, or **On hold**, and sort by recent, oldest, or fit. Cards show **Mission Fit**, **Reachability**, **Readiness**, and **Permission**. Statuses are Planning, Active, Paused, Completed, and Archived.

## Work a mission

Open a mission for:

- **Priority targets** and **People you should meet for this mission**
- **Best paths**
- **Recent activity** and **Upcoming milestones**
- a suggested next step with **Request introduction** or **Open path**
- **Mission intelligence**, which is labeled **Beta**

Choose **Pause mission** or **Resume mission** to change the mission's state. After your network changes, a mission can briefly show an out-of-date status while it re-evaluates.

Adding collaborators is not available yet. You own the missions you create. You can also attach a [collection](/workspace/collections) as a mission's targets.

## Continue safely

Open the related path, verify the target and connector, and edit the draft before any external communication. If evidence is missing, sync more network data instead of assuming a path exists.
