The app route named **Connections** summarizes your current relationship graph. Open it from the Network Health card or use the link below.

[Open Connections](https://app.getintrod.ai/network)

## What you can review

- contacts and represented companies
- warm-path and strong-connector counts
- top connectors based on available contact and introduction records
- top companies represented in synced contacts
- recently discovered paths derived from introduction records
- LinkedIn sync health and data-quality counts
- Google, Outlook, and community connection status

Relationship-intelligence metrics appear only when the workspace has enough graph evidence. Empty states mean Introd does not have the corresponding record yet.

## Use Connections

1. Check **LinkedIn sync health** and **Network health** first.
2. If a source is pending, partial, failed, or not connected, select **Manage sync**.
3. Review **Top connectors** and **Recently discovered paths**.
4. Open a contact, company, or introduction to verify the underlying evidence before acting.

The search box filters the people used in the top-connector view. Use **People** or **Company** for full search.

## Read LinkedIn sync health

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Synced | Introd has a completed or usable LinkedIn sync record. |
| Partial | Some visible records may not yet be imported, or removal checks were skipped. |
| Pending | The sync has not completed or is still running. |
| Failed | The latest sync reports an error. |

Use the detailed counts to distinguish visible, imported, incomplete, and removed records.
